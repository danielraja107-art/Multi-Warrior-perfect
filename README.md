# ⚔️ Storm Arena

A real-time online multiplayer 3D arena beat-'em-up where players team up, fight waves of enemies, collect weapons, and defeat powerful bosses.

> **Project:** Multi-Warrior / Storm Arena  
> **Type:** Online Multiplayer 3D Action Game  
> **Players:** 2–4 players  
> **Status:** In Development

---

## 🎮 About the Game

Storm Arena is a cooperative multiplayer fighting game built around close-range combat.

Players enter a dangerous arena and work together to survive increasingly difficult enemy waves. The game focuses on fast movement, melee combat, weapons, teamwork, enemy AI, and boss battles.

The goal is simple:

**Fight. Survive. Level up. Defeat the boss.**

---

## 🕹️ Gameplay

Players join an online room and enter the arena together.

The gameplay loop is:

```text
Join / Create Room
        ↓
Enter Arena
        ↓
Fight Enemy Waves
        ↓
Collect / Use Weapons
        ↓
Survive Increasing Difficulty
        ↓
Fight Boss
        ↓
Victory / Defeat
        ↓
XP & Progression
